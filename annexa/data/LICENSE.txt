annexa-obf-derivative-2026-10-10.csv.gz

Derived from the Open Beauty Facts database (dump of 2026-10-10).
Source: https://world.openbeautyfacts.org/data

Database: Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0
  https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/
Contents of individual records: Database Contents License (DbCL) 1.0
  https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/dbcl/1-0/

Changes made: records without an ingredient list were removed; the
retained columns are code, product_name, brands, countries_en,
ingredients_text and last_modified; whitespace was collapsed and
duplicate barcodes dropped. No product images are included.

This file is published to satisfy the ODbL share-alike obligation for
the Derivative Database embedded in the Annexa iOS app. It contains no
CosIng data; the EU regulatory data shipped alongside it is CC BY 4.0
and is distributed as a separate file.
