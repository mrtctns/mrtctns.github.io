Privacy Policy

Applies to every app published by Mert Çetin. Last updated 26 October 2026.

The short version

Most of these apps have no accounts and no servers of their own. What you do in them stays on your device. The personal data that leaves your phone is what Google's advertising SDK collects in the apps that show ads, listed below, and what Deprem Uyarı Sistemi: Anlık sends to its own server so it can notify you while the app is closed, described in its own section.

What stays on the device

Except where an app's own section says otherwise, location, saved items, settings, history, photos, audio recordings and sensor readings are held on the device and are not uploaded. Apps that use location do so to sort or filter content locally. Apps that record audio or use the camera process it on the device unless their own screens say otherwise. Deleting the app deletes this data.

What the apps download

Some apps fetch public data files — for example Sea Temperature downloads a daily temperature feed. These are plain file requests. Nothing about you is sent with them beyond what any web request carries.

English

This app is the exception to the general rule on this page: to send you a personal notification while the app is closed, it sends a small amount of data to our own server. There is no account, no advertising, and no analytics or crash-reporting tool.

What goes to the server

DataWhat for
Location, rounded to two decimals (≈ 1.1 km): the single point alerts are worked out for (the device's location or the city you pick) Working out how far an earthquake is from you and the shaking expected at your location, and sending the alert to you personally.
The Apple Push Notification service (APNs) device token Delivering the notification to this device.
Alert preferences: intensity threshold, Turkey-wide threshold, quiet hours and time zone, language, alert sound Sending according to your choices and holding alerts back during quiet hours.
Purchase status: trial / subscription / lifetime, expiry date, and the originalTransactionId Apple assigns to the purchase Checking your right to alerts and processing cancellations and refunds from Apple's server notifications. Your card details and Apple Account never reach us.
A hash (SHA-256) of a secret generated per installation Making sure only your device can reach your record; moving your record to you when the device token changes (new phone, restore from backup) and stopping anyone else from taking it over. The secret itself is not stored, disappears when you delete the app, and does not follow you across installations.
Registration and last-seen times, trial start/end, which earthquake was notified to you and when Not sending the same alert twice, limiting alert frequency, sending each trial reminder once.
Server access log: time, requested address (the device token is masked) and response code. IP addresses and request headers are not written to the log. Diagnosing faults. Kept in size-limited rotating files.
A counter derived from the IP address, to limit fake registrations Limiting how many new registrations come from one address. The counter lives in memory only, the address is hashed with a salt that changes daily; it is not written to disk or to the log and is wiped when the server restarts.

What we do not send

Name, email, phone, account, advertising identifier (IDFA), exact coordinates, movement history, contacts, photos. Location is not tracked in the background; it is read once, only while the app is open and when you tap.

How long, and how to delete it

  • If a device is not seen for 90 days its record is deleted. When Apple reports the token invalid (the app was deleted) the record is deleted straight away.
  • Settings › Delete my data: your record on the server and the delivery records for this device are genuinely deleted, not flagged. Your trial resets; an active subscription is not canceled and comes back with Restore purchases.
  • In server backups a deleted record can remain until the backup cycle completes, for at most 30 days. Access-log lines disappear with the rotating files.

Who it is shared with

We share data with no advertising, analytics or data-broker third party, and we sell none. Technically we rely on two infrastructures: Apple (notification delivery and purchase verification) and the cloud hosting service our server runs on (Amazon Web Services, Seoul region, South Korea). For the earthquake list and map your phone connects directly to the open data servers of AFAD, EMSC and USGS and to Apple Maps; those requests carry your IP address to those services, not your location.

Your rights

Deletion is done inside the app. For access, correction or deletion requests write to mrtctns22@gmail.com; we need your device token to find the record, so the Delete my data button is the easiest route. For rights under Turkey's Law No. 6698 (KVKK) you can write to the same address. Data controller: Mert Çetin.

Advertising

Apps that show ads use Google AdMob. Where an app does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission, it requests non-personalised ads and no advertising identifier is used. AdMob may collect:

  • a device identifier, and advertising interaction data;
  • coarse (city-level) location;
  • app usage and diagnostic data, including crash reports.

This is used to deliver and measure ads and to keep the SDK working. See Google's privacy policy. Each app's App Store listing shows exactly what it collects under "App Privacy".

Purchases

Subscriptions and in-app purchases are processed by Apple. Apple tells the app whether a purchase is active; it does not share your payment details, and we never see them. The one exception to "Apple tells the app" is Deprem Uyarı Sistemi: Anlık, whose server also verifies the purchase with Apple; what it keeps is listed in its section.

Children

These apps are not directed at children under 13 and do not knowingly collect data from them.

Your rights

For Deprem Uyarı Sistemi: Anlık, delete your server record in the app (Settings › Delete my data) or write to us. For advertising data held by Google, use Google's own controls.
