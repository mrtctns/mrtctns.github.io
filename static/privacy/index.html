Privacy Policy

Applies to every app published by Mert Çetin. Last updated 6 August 2026.

The short version

These apps have no accounts and no servers of their own. What you do in them stays on your device. The only personal data that leaves your phone is what Google's advertising SDK collects in the apps that show ads, and that is listed below.

What stays on the device

Location, saved items, settings, history, photos, audio recordings and sensor readings are held on the device and are not uploaded. Apps that use location do so to sort or filter content locally. Apps that record audio or use the camera process it on the device unless their own screens say otherwise. Deleting the app deletes this data.

What the apps download

Some apps fetch public data files — for example Sea Temperature downloads a daily temperature feed. These are plain file requests. Nothing about you is sent with them beyond what any web request carries.

Advertising

Apps that show ads use Google AdMob. Where an app does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission, it requests non-personalised ads and no advertising identifier is used. AdMob may collect:

  • a device identifier, and advertising interaction data;
  • coarse (city-level) location;
  • app usage and diagnostic data, including crash reports.

This is used to deliver and measure ads and to keep the SDK working. See Google's privacy policy. Each app's App Store listing shows exactly what it collects under "App Privacy".

Purchases

Subscriptions and in-app purchases are processed by Apple. Apple tells the app whether a purchase is active; it does not share your payment details, and we never see them.

Children

These apps are not directed at children under 13 and do not knowingly collect data from them.

Your rights

Because there is no account and no server holding your data, there is nothing for us to export or delete on request — removing the app removes it. For advertising data held by Google, use Google's own controls. Questions: mertcetin817@hotmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
